---
Title: "Am I really lagging behind in this Generative AI craze?"
Date: "2026-08-04"
---

People already know I am a self-taught computer programmer. I am an avid reader and listener. I like to figure things out by myself and understand how they come to be by their most fundamental aspects. This is one of the things in which I find joy in life and I know there is so much value in approaching things that way even though it often take time or rather happen in time shifts that doesn't quite fit the speed in which business happen these days. That method isn't necessarily valued by the current economy and it gives way to the marginalization of folks who possess a better intuition about how things stay together interacting and how they may unfold in the future. This pattern is crushed only when those that acquire knowledge in that way come across or come up with something unexpected that disrupt the pace of history and open up possibilities that fall outside established trends. Now let me tell you how I've been using AI.

A few months after ChatGPT broke out, I believe it was GPT 3.5 or something, a tricky problem arrived to me as part of a user story I was working on for a client project. After spending a few days with it I realized I was able to describe a potential solution fairly well in words but still I had little clue about how to implement it in code so I decided to give it to ChatGPT. The result shocked me instantly. Up until that moment I had only heard about other people experiences. I was hesitating to give it try without really having a use case for it and once that opportunity arrived I gave it a go and it blew me away. From that moment onwards I realized software development would not evolve the same way it had for the past couple of decades. In fact I was convinced no computer aided job would be the same ever again.

That was the last time I asked an LLM to write code for me and it has been that way ever since. During the months following it I saw all kinds of buzzwords around AI appear in the coding industry. Vibe-coding, MCP, agents, spec-driven development and many others showed up while I remained an outsider. I saw how some of the ideas came into fashion and then disappeared and as a JS specialist acquainted with the so called "JS fatigue" it didn't surprised me at all. Yes, I was uncomfortable. It isn't nice to feel left behind and then eventually be left without a job. However I had an hunch telling me I shouldn't jump on the fad and furthermore AI was already entering my daily workflow without asking for permission.

As with every other developer of my generation, Internet search engines, particularly Google, have been core to my success in the market. I googled my way into learning and practicing the skills required to execute the tasks I got paid to complete. It was the natural way to do things. The google search engine helped me find books, articles, videos, courses, bootcamps, community forums, meetups, newsletters, chat rooms, jobs and so on, all of them fundamental to my career development. The one thing though that Google wasn't able to provide me with timely and at my will was someone else more knowledgeable than I to pair with whenever I want. A mentor, an oracle or guide I could come to ask questions with my limited vocabulary. It wasn't until it finally was.

Generative AI opened the doors to motivated, self-driven and curious people like me to explore information and acquire knowledge effectively without limits. The fact that you can come to any LLM chat bot and ask questions as if you were having a Socratic dialogue with some higher intelligence is really where the revolution humanity is bound to go through exists in my opinion. Now these crazy capabilities doesn't come with major challenges such us misinformation or worse, disinformation, and that's where the classic tools and techniques for acquiring knowledge which folks of my generation had access to through traditional education come into play.

Being able to think critically and put to test assumptions to identify and correct biases is something that no AI model is able to do by itself nowadays and is arguably something that it will ever be able to do. People who is neck deep in the AI craze right now should stop for a moment and really think about how the world is set to change over the next few years. The technological advances and the pace and ways in which they are shaping the planet earth are putting the humanity in front of something major now and as finite, emotionally capable beings like we humans are, I'd rather go through all of this in my own pace to be caught up by opportunities than chase them outside my humane boundaries which I learned to be in peace with as I approach my 4th decade alive. Over the next years AI will continue to evolve and transform the world and I'll be following it closely. Time is our most precious asset and we have to be wise using it.
